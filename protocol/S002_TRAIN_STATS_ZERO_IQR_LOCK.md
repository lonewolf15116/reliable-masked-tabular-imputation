# S002 — Adult training-only feature statistics and exact-zero-IQR lock

Recorded 2026-11-07. **Authorized G1A audit preparation only.** S001 source checksum and train-index SHA must pass before running. No imputation, model fitting, masking, or test evaluation is permitted.

Use exactly the committed S001 train indices (1-indexed raw-file row IDs); do not re-split. Read original Adult bytes only after matching SHA-256; downstream field parsing strips surrounding whitespace and leaves `?` an **observed categorical level**, not null. `income` is a stratification label but is **excluded from all imputation feature sets**. No validation/test feature values may be used for feature-statistic estimation, constant determination, category vocabulary construction, or scaling.

For each of the six original Adult numeric predictors (`age`, `fnlwgt`, `education-num`, `capital-gain`, `capital-loss`, `hours-per-week`), compute NumPy 2.1.3 `np.quantile(values, [0.25,0.5,0.75], method="linear")` on **training rows only**. IQR is Q75-Q25 **without rounding or tolerance**. A feature is exact-zero-IQR iff this difference equals numeric 0.0. Positive but small IQR is NOT excluded. Save n, quartiles, IQR and observed min/max, plus all training categorical vocabularies (literal `?` retained) to `artifacts/stats/adult_train_feature_stats.json`. Calculate SHA of raw Adult source and S001 train indices; include both in result. Never modify an existing stats artifact with generate mode; verify mode recomputes all results and byte-compares.

**Metric policy:** Numeric features with IQR==0 are **excluded from the primary aggregate IQR-normalized error numerator and denominator**, and reported **separately** with their raw-unit error and feature-specific metrics. Record the count and names. They are NOT dropped as columns/rows from the raw dataset; whether to mask them for separate analysis must be declared in the later eligible-cell manifest. Categorical `?` stays observed and eligible subject to the later frozen cell rules. All methods share the same eventually frozen eligible population.

The S002 artifact commits the measured zero-IQR list. A regression verification test SHALL fail whenever a source file, train index file, the IQR computation, or the exact zero-IQR set differs from the committed artifact. Future intentional changes require formal protocol amendment and renewed gate approval. No new data-dependent floor is picked here: scale of exact-zero-IQR columns is undefined for primary aggregation, and separate raw-unit summaries do not divide by IQR.
