## Why scope changes

The objective is no longer to optimise protocol process before first evidence. Existing simulations exposed failures of downstream binary non-inferiority methods, leading to A001 descriptive-only scope. No imputation results have yet been generated. Do not reopen NI gate design, run another methods-gate, or claim novelty from those failures.

Scientific hypothesis: **uncertainty-guided selective imputation might lower accepted-cell error and human review cost at matched coverage versus uncertainty-naive and incumbent baselines**. Full-coverage RMSE is a diagnostic, not the main endpoint.

## Separate feasibility pilot P001 — authorization boundary

P001 may be **authorized only after the minimal G1A closeout is signed**; it is not G1B/G1C or an early G1D test evaluation. Its result is exploratory and cannot serve as confirmatory evidence. Adult 32,561 original rows, immutable S001 training and validation only; no S001 test row may be read or used for pilot evaluation, tuning, uncertainty calibration or final conclusions. One prespecified 20% fixed-count MAR regime using the approved single `age` anchor; `age` never MAR masked. Use identical frozen masks/cells for all methods. Compare (i) MICE, (ii) MissForest, (iii) minimal masked-feature MLP with a small independently initialized ensemble for risk estimation. Fit preprocessing and all imputers on training data only. Use a fixed validation separation for calibrator/threshold selection vs reported pilot curve to avoid evaluating on the same examples used for selection; alternatively use separate cross-fit folds predeclared before running. No use of `income` as input to any imputer. No external datasets.

Outcomes at **matched accepted-cell coverage, especially 80%**: risk/error on the accepted 80%; selective-risk versus coverage curve; ranking/uncertainty diagnostics; featurewise interval or set calibration and empirical coverage if applicable; simulated review fraction and cost at a **declared** unit review cost. Full-coverage numeric RMSE and categorical error are secondary. Report numeric and categorical errors separately rather than mixing incomparable units. Zero-IQR numeric features separately, not divided by zero. Distinguish prediction-set calibration from point-error risk ranking. A naive random-abstention baseline must be included for interpretability; any uncertainty score produced by MICE/MissForest must be specified and validated rather than invented.

Choose computational ceilings before any fit (one development-only condition, finite seeds, brief budget). Negative outcomes and method failures must be fully reported. Avoid claims of non-inferiority, causal effect, general MAR coverage, or scientific novelty. A pilot success is a signal to fund full G1B and G1C; a failure triggers stop/pivot review, not automatic abandonment or protocol repair loops.

Continue to full research only if selective-risk improvement relative to strongest baseline at matched 80% coverage is practically meaningful under a **declared review-cost model** and not an artifact of feature mix, leakage, or masking. If MLP loses, ask whether baseline uncertainty ranking, calibration and abstention still offer a viable contribution; do not require the MLP to beat MissForest on raw RMSE. Record all null and negative outcomes.

## A004 owner-accepted P001 design constraints — planned, not executed

Accepted 2026-11-07; final **P001 preregistration must be frozen after G1A sign-off and before any pilot fitting or masking**. This addendum captures decisions and required specification without creating or claiming a held-out hash. It supersedes A004's generic "either split or cross-fit" wording for this pilot.

**Validation split, Option A exclusively:** The locked S001 validation partition of 4,884 row IDs is split into **2,442 selection** and **2,442 reporting** IDs using an independently named `pilot_validation_partition` seed distinct from S001 split/mask/model/calibration/bootstrap seeds. Final preregistration must pin seed, randomization algorithm/library versions, source S001 validation-index SHA, exact sorted 1-index row-ID files and their SHA-256 hashes **before first model fit**. Selection rows may guide MLP early stopping, ensemble selection, hyperparameters, calibrators, and 80%-auto-accept rule, including any tie-breaking algorithm; reporting rows may only be used once for a frozen evaluation. Explicitly prohibit looking at held-out score distributions to select rank cutoffs, metrics, thresholds, feature subsets or calibrator. To achieve 80% coverage on held-out data, report the realized coverage of the selection-frozen numerical threshold; an additional 80%-coverage rank slice on held-out may be plotted **as descriptive curve interpolation**, not called a prespecified fixed-threshold policy. Report selection vs held-out differences instead of repairing them after looking.

**Baseline and MLP risk scoring:** MICE uses empirical per-cell variance across a prospectively frozen number `m` of imputations and clearly documents how multiple stochastic draws are obtained and categorical uncertainties represented. MissForest uses per-cell tree-prediction dispersion from a reproducible implementation and forest settings; it is not safe to assume a library wrapper exports per-tree estimates. MLP uses dispersion from **five independently initialized** masked-training ensemble members. Normalize any numeric risk scores using **training-only** scales when compared across features; do not pretend raw variance is commensurate across heterogeneous numeric columns or categorical levels. Define categorical risk (e.g., disagreement / category probability uncertainty) separately for each method before pilot execution. Include a random-abstention comparator. If a baseline risk implementation is unavailable or fails validation, explicitly report **not comparable**; do not quietly treat it as random risk.

**Architecture and scope:** Minimum viable three-hidden-layer masked MLP with width/embedding dimension 64, ensemble size 5, fixed architecture and mask-during-training; no transformer, architecture search or post-hoc training extensions. All preprocessing fit only on frozen S001 training data, no `income` as imputation feature, no S001 test rows accessed. A bounded compute ceiling, number of missingness mask seeds and `m` must be fixed in final P001 preregistration. Target is 20% Adult MAR, conditional on a single never-masked `age` anchor, if A003 is approved.

**Primary loss comparison:** Specify **one** shared dimensionless primary accepted-cell error (numeric, IQR-normalized where IQR>0; categorical evaluation separately or normalized via a preregistered weighting rule) *before execution*; no arbitrary numeric/categorical pooling after inspecting results. At the fixed **selection-derived** 80% target, compare held-out realized coverage and accepted-cell loss against whichever of MICE/MissForest has **lower held-out accepted-cell loss at matched coverage**, reporting both individual baselines to avoid selective cherry-picking. For selective-risk curves at exact matched 80% fraction, compare methods using a fixed prespecified tie-break rule; distinguish this *oracle-by-ranking reporting diagnostic* from deployed thresholds learned on selection data.

**Calibration:** If reporting 90% predictive intervals for numeric features or 90%-nominal prediction sets for categorical features, fit calibration on the selection half only, report empirical held-out featurewise coverage, and predeclare treatment of features with insufficient samples. No assumption that ensemble variance alone is a calibrated interval. The provisional GO calibration band is **85%–95% empirical coverage for nominal 90%**, with clear separate numeric and categorical tests; this band is a heuristic pilot decision threshold, not a formal guarantee.

**Review-queue simulation:** No labour-hours claims. For each method, freeze an ordering from its risk score and compute the number/fraction of held-out masked cells requiring human review (not auto-accepted) to reach the **MLP's realized accepted-cell error at its selection-frozen approximately 80%-coverage policy**, using the prespecified primary loss. A review is simulated as perfect correction **only for queue accounting**; no observed human costs or labour savings inferred. If a baseline cannot attain that accepted-cell error at any permissible nonempty accepted fraction, record **unattainable**. Disclose operating-point variance and the fact that using held-out errors to find the comparator's matching point makes this a retrospective diagnostic, not a deployed threshold-selection rule.

**Preliminary quantitative go/pivot/stop (to be copied into final P001 preregistration before any fitting):**
- **GO:** MLP held-out accepted-cell loss at exactly matched 80% coverage is at least **5% lower** than the stronger of MICE and MissForest (relative reduction using baseline as denominator), **and** every in-scope nominal-90% calibration metric passes the preregistered 85%–95% band, **and** its review queue is strictly shorter than the strongest baseline's queue at the same target accepted-cell loss. Require all three.
- **PIVOT:** GO not met; MLP not better on accepted-cell loss, but **either** calibration is better under the preregistered distance-to-nominal metric **or** queue length is smaller. State any unusable/not-comparable baseline uncertainties instead of counting absence as an MLP win.
- **STOP for this MLP hypothesis:** GO not met; MLP fails accepted-cell loss and wins neither calibration nor review-queue length. Does not falsify all possible imputation models or broader MAR settings.
- If results do not fit these three cases (e.g. loss improvement <5% with better calibration but no queue benefit, indeterminate calibration, or a baseline implementation failure), declare **INCONCLUSIVE / REVISE**, not forced GO. The primary outcome remains exploratory; small held-out differences do not establish novelty or generality.
