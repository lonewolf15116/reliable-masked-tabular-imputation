## Independence and exact-enumeration amendment (2026-11-07)

A same-author rewrite of the sampler or a reference function authored by the project team is **not an independent verification**. Require a separately sourced and provenance-recorded specification/reference implementation, or independent human review of the exact finite-set probabilities and test harness, with reviewer/source identity and version/hash recorded **before** treating R002's distributional check as independently verified. An internal analytic calculation may be valuable and recorded as `INTERNAL_CROSSCHECK`, but it cannot silently satisfy the `INDEPENDENT` gate.

Mathematical feasibility correction: **100 rows with 10 masked cells is not enumerable** (100 choose 10 = 17,310,309,456,440). For exact reference choose, in advance of looking at sampling results, **n=10, k=2, one feature (Adult `occupation`), rate=0.20**, giving just 45 unordered masks, using deterministic train-row selection defined by split-order positions `[0, 1, ..., 9]` in the sorted train-index file. If this selection has a degenerate anchor distribution, record it and use a separately predeclared spread selection (e.g. 10 evenly spaced train positions) rather than outcome-based substitutions. A full 100-row subset may be checked for counts/performance only, never called enumerated.

Reference law is weighted *Gumbel-top-k* (equivalently Plackett-Luce sequential probability-proportional-to-weight draws without replacement) with weights `p_i` from the clipped-logistic function. For a specific unordered k-subset S, probability is the sum over its k! ordered permutations of `prod_t w_{i_t}/(W-sum_{u<t} w_{i_u})`. These weights are **not** fixed-count marginal inclusion probabilities. The full-Adult checks and small-n frequency check are distinct. The frozen R002 execution receipt must explicitly state independence provenance and avoid passing the gate solely because an internal reference matched.
